- Severity: Critical when deterministic programme dispatch guards dependency or
  review order.
- Status: CORRECTION REVIEW in `phase5-programme-dispatch-correction`. The first
  independent review rejected this record's provisional route/mutation count;
  one focused recheck remains. P5.1 is accepted; P5.2 product work remains
  blocked until independent and root dual PASS.
- Incident: six failed, stopped, or superseded Phase 4 review routes remained
  `blocked` inside the executable `stages` list after later owner-authorized
  recovery and accepted Phase 4 final review. `CURRENT.md` and the programme
  pointed at P5.2, while the deterministic dispatcher correctly stopped at the
  earlier `phase4-review`. Separately, three declared Phase 5 kinds had no exact
  dispatcher route, while the existing `correction` and
  `implementation_sequence` routes still emitted the retired Terra identifier

  medium. The defect therefore covers five Phase 5 route changes: three missing
  and two misrouted.
- Violated invariant: executable stages contain only the current serial
  acceptance chain. Failed attempts remain immutable historical evidence with
  explicit non-acceptance; a current stage is the first unfinished executable
  dependency; every dispatchable kind has one exact model/effort route and every
  unknown kind refuses.
- Why prior evidence missed it: architecture and repository contract checks
  validated contiguous order, immediate dependencies, current/capsule shape,
  and standard phase routes without invoking the real dispatcher against the
  tracked programme. Dispatcher tests also encoded Terra as the expected
  correction/implementation route. Later accepted descendants made the state
  look coherent, but no check intersected the complete non-accepted stage set
  with executable history or the complete Phase 5 kind/model/effort set with
  `STAGE_ROUTES`.
- False assumption: a failed row could remain `blocked` in the executable list
  as durable history, later accepted rows plus a manually advanced
  `current_stage_id` were enough to distinguish history from authority, and a
  route was usable merely because its kind existed even when its model was the
  retired Terra identifier.
- Prevention invariant: move the complete failed/stopped/superseded universe to
