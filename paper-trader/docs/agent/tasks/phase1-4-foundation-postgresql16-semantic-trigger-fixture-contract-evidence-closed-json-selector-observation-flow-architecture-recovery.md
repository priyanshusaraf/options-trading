This capsule replaces the exhausted derived-flow root authority. It preserves
the complete source-derived mechanic-flow and typed namespaced language, and it
adds an explicit non-observation boundary: requested and authority-derived
identity may select a declassified mechanic but may not appear in exceptions,
logs, diagnostics, receipts, hashes, formatting, or any other observation.
