## Confirmed causes and correction seams

The scroll defect is caused by the collapsed CSS rule on the same mounted
`.palette-pane`: its normal `overflow-y: auto` becomes `overflow: hidden`.
Chromium reset `scrollTop` from `120` to `0` in the authenticated predecessor
receipt. The query, disclosure state, and canvas-width gain already passed.
The successor must keep the current `58px` collapsed grid track, hidden
`.palette-content`, and `overflow: hidden`; before closing it records the pane's
current `scrollTop`, and after the reopen commit it restores that value in a
layout-timed effect before paint. It must not unmount content, reserve the
expanded palette width, or reveal hidden palette content.

The shortcut defect is caused by `isEditableTarget` accepting only an
`HTMLElement` target and calling `matches(...)` on that target alone. A nested
`span` therefore does not match its contenteditable ancestor. The successor
must use `Element`-safe inspection of `event.target.closest(...)` plus editable
elements in `event.composedPath()` so light-DOM ancestors and open-shadow event
paths are refused. The editable selector remains `input`, `textarea`, `select`,
effective contenteditable other than explicit `false`, and `[role="textbox"]`.
Existing composition, repeat, Ctrl, Alt, Meta, direct-input, and plain
`Shift+A` behavior remains unchanged.
