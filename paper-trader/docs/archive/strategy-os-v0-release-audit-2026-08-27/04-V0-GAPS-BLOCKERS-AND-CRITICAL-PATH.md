```text
       |
       +--> V0-D Zerodha/static-scope/options/VIX/order-flow data spine
       +--> V0-E chart/annotation/replay spine
       +--> V0-F complete robustness lab
       |
       v
V0-G monitoring/signals/review
       |
       v
V0-H Precision Slate convergence and golden journey
       |
       v
V0-I security/operations/deployability/analytics
       |
       v
V0-J A/B/D/E/F + owner strategies + final V0 review
```

Machine phase gates remain serial. Inside a V0 stage, data, UI fixtures and
independent oracle lanes may run in parallel only after shared contracts freeze.

## Why this is faster than the old plan

The old V1 critical path required capital admission, Dynamic Watchlists, provider
coherence, chart proposal/approval, controlled execution, full frontend, operations
and A–F release evidence. Phase 5 capital work is already preserved. Accelerated V0:

- removes Dynamic Watchlists from public V0;
- removes order, fill, capital, approval and reconciliation behavior from public V0;
- uses one provider/data connection;
- preserves but does not certify execution;
- moves research UX, monitoring and beta operations ahead of V1 execution work;
- evaluates A/B/D/E/F at research/signal eligibility only.

It does not reduce indicator accuracy, data truth, causality, tenancy, secret,
licence, deployment or reproducibility standards.

## Release gates

### Gate 0 — owner dogfood

Must pass V0-A, the node allowlist subset needed by the tested strategy, one canonical
builder/backtest path, implemented robustness methods, mock/read-only data and exact
local persistence. No real-user onboarding claim.

### Gate A — demo ready

One complete golden path on controlled approved data, no execution surface, exact
build identity, truthful Unknown states and reproducible reset. Benchmark A is the
minimum vertical; B/D/E/F may be staged demonstrations only when their data exists.

### Gate B — founder-assisted private beta

V0-A through V0-I, 5–15 user-specific Zerodha connections, quotas, backups,
monitoring, incident/support process, privacy/terms, legal/data/chart approvals and
at least A plus the V0 features actually exposed. No missing feature may be shown as
working.

### Gate C — paid private beta

Gate B plus billing/refund/support terms, paid-product chart and market-data rights,
counsel-approved positioning/performance language, retention/deletion, measured
capacity and reliable recovery.

### Gate D — broader public beta

Gate C plus self-service onboarding, abuse/rate controls, broader capacity,
operational on-call/incident practice, product analytics, support tooling and
production rehearsal.

## September 8 critical path

Eleven days from audit close cannot produce the full requested V0 from current
evidence. The honest target is:

1. controlled owner demo of current mock graph/backtest/research screens;
2. reviewed V0 architecture and first execution slice;
3. observed owner strategy workflow documenting friction;
4. no beta, paid or data/licence claim.

The audit does not convert this into a product deadline. External approvals and data
collection can keep B/D/E/F unbounded until resolved.

## Items that must not block V0

- Live/certified execution.
- Dynamic Watchlists.
- Multiple brokers.
- Portfolio capital allocation.
- Arbitrary Python and packages.
- Team workspaces/RBAC beyond safe beta administration.
- Public sharing/marketplace/copy trading.
- Customer-facing AI.
- Fundamentals terminal, general workflows or institutional integration.
