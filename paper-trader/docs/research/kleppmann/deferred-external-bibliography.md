# Deferred direct external bibliography

The [machine-readable link set](deferred-external-links.jsonl) records all 2,016
direct external URLs discovered by the first-party HTML traversal and their source
pages. The [triage overlay](one-hop-triage.jsonl) classifies every record without
changing the crawl lineage: 117 bounded candidates, 454 other deferred artifacts
and 1,445 bibliography/context records.

Three queue records received selected review in this capsule: the Cambridge notes,
Hermitage and the Redlock counteranalysis. Five first-party or supplemental exact
artifacts are tracked in `licensed-artifact-reviews.json`. All other triaged links
remain unread unless another explicit review record says otherwise.

Do not recursively crawl the wider internet. PDF annotation links and further
links inside selected external course artifacts remain outside this bounded pass.
