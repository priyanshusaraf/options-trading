## Earliest safe restart decisions

- Research may resume only from a checkpoint whose graph, registry, data, policy, ResourcePlan, reset reason, and implementation closure reconstruct exactly.
- Broker activity may resume only after all prior submitted intents and protection orders are reconciled for the exact owner/account/connection/deployment scope.
- Capital admission may resume only after held reservations, commands, unresolved intents, capital, margin, and head revision form one fresh snapshot.
- Realtime clients may reconnect from a scoped cursor, but must reload durable state when retention requires resync.

## Restore evidence deliberately not claimed

No PostgreSQL server, container, production backup, credential, provider, live account, VPS, or deployment path was used. The matrix therefore rejects repository-wide “recoverable,” “HA,” or “production-ready” labels.
